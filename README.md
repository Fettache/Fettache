## About Me

I am a first-year engineering student at ENSIASD, specializing in Data Science, Big Data and AI. I hold a University Diploma of Technology (DUT) in Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies from EST Meknes.

My academic and professional projects include Machine Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, IoT security and data applications. During my internship at e-acta, I worked on a regulatory RAG prototype. My final-year project, completed in a pair, focused on detecting anomalies in IoT/MQTT traffic using Machine Learning.

I am especially interested in:

- Machine Learning and Deep Learning;
- Data Science, Big Data and Data Engineering;
- NLP, Generative AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG);
- Computer Vision and anomaly detection;
- IoT security and real-time intelligent applications.

## Experience & Awards

- **AI/Data Internship — e-acta, Fes:** Contributed to a regulatory RAG module for actaDiag, covering multi-source document ingestion, semantic search and evidence-grounded responses. The project used PostgreSQL/pgvector and local embeddings.
- **3rd Prize — RamadanIA Hackathon 2026:** Our Tech Team presented Trippple, an AI-assisted travel-planning platform, and received the **Impact Territorial & Utilité Publique** award.
